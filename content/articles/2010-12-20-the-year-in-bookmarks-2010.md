As you may have heard, [Delicious is going away](http://techcrunch.com/2010/12/16/is-yahoo-shutting-down-del-icio-us/), or at least [being sold off](http://techcrunch.com/2010/12/17/yahoo-trying-to-sell-del-icio-us-not-to-shut-it-down/), or otherwise being destroyed by Yahoo. In the last 72 hours, I’ve tried out the two leading alternatives, [Diigo](https://diigo.com/) and [pinboard](http://pinboard.in/). Along the way, I got to wondering why I keep all these bookmarks around. I do often search back for things. And in paging through my 1173 bookmarks from 2010, they formed an interesting lens through which to look back at the year. And so I present 2010, the year in bookmarks.
