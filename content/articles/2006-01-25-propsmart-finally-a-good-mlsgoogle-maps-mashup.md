I just happened to catch a Google ad for [propsmart](http://propsmart.com), and I’m glad that I did. I enjoy regularly browsing real estate listings in both Michigan and Massachusetts. The web-based browsing systems have been gradually improving, but none of them have yet got it right. [propsmart](http://propsmart.com) is a big step in the right direction. For those of you who have seen [Housing Maps](http://housingmaps.com), this is basically housing maps applied to MLS and several other data sources, rather than just [Craigslist](http://craigslist.org).

In particular, all the previous services I have used have been attached to one brokerage or another, and have considered it their solemn duty to exclude FSBO properties and maintain the real estate agent oligopoly. A service like propsmart, or a similar one, has the possibility to greatly improve the FSBO market, and improve information flow in the real estate market in general.

[propsmart](http://propsmart.com) is far from perfect. But it is much better than anything I’ve seen. Check it out.
